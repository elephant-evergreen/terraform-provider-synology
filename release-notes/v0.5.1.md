## Upgrade notes

Existing configurations and state need no changes. Re-apply any permission change that omitted a principal; v0.5.0 failed at the final readback instead of completing the removal.

## Fixes

- `synology_share_permissions` now removes a principal that is omitted from the policy. v0.5.0 applied reductions and denies before grants but iterated only over the desired map, so a removed principal kept its access and the final readback rejected the incomplete policy.
